Nuke + Python tools, artist support, and production compositing.

I’m a VFX compositor building toward artist-facing Nuke tools, pipeline support, and production technology work. I like solving the workflow problems that slow artists down: heavy scripts, broken paths, unclear handoffs, repeated setup work, and tools that are hard to trust.

My strongest work sits between the artist side and the technical side: understanding what a shot or workflow needs, finding the source of the problem, and applying a safe fix that helps people keep moving.
